3. What this license permits, and on what conditions

A build may be called Verdetto, use the icon or wordmark, or show the "Built on Verdetto" badge only with prior written approval under this license, and only while it meets all of the following.

Product standards (quality control):

  1. It passes the published Verdetto conformance suite for the version it is built from: the symbology matrix, the precision gates and the safety-list checks, with the results recorded.
  2. It ships no proprietary advertising, analytics or telemetry library, and no change that disables a check, a warning or a notice the original shows.
  3. Verdetto may request a copy of any build that carries the marks and review it against these terms at any time, and keeps a record of approvals and reviews.

Values:

  1. No advertising. No ads of any kind, no sponsored content, no promoted results, no interstitials, no "remove ads" purchase.
  2. No tracking. No analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers or fingerprinting. Nothing leaves the device except the lookups the user can see and switch off, as the privacy policy describes them.
  3. Everything works for free. Every scan, decode and check is free for every user, with no feature gate, trial, subscription or paywall. A voluntary support purchase that unlocks nothing needed to use the app is allowed.
  4. An honest interface. No fake buttons, no ads dressed as controls, no forced or timed prompts, no rating or share nags, no pre-ticked consent, no dark patterns.
  5. No safety claims. Never tell a user a code, link or site is safe, secure, protected, verified or clean. Show what was checked and what was found; the user decides.
  6. Notices kept. Copyright and license notices, third-party attributions and the source-code link stay in the app and its listing, as the code licenses require.
